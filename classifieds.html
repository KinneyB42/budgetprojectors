How Buying and Selling Works Here

BudgetProjectors provides this board as a community service only. BudgetProjectors is not a party to any transaction, does not handle payments, and does not verify sellers, buyers, or the condition of any item listed. All transactions are solely between the buyer and the seller.

For your protection: use a payment method that offers buyer and seller protection, such as PayPal Goods and Services, and never send a Friends and Family payment to someone you do not know. For local sales, meet in a public place. Sellers, describe your item honestly, including lamp hours and any known faults. Listings may be declined or removed at any time.

How to Post a Listing

Listings are free. Send an email with the following information, and attach clear photos of the item, including a photo of the projector powered on and projecting an image where applicable:

1. What you are selling, including the exact model name
2. Condition, and for projectors the lamp or light source hours
3. Any known faults, damage, or missing accessories
4. Your asking price
5. Your city and state
6. Whether you offer shipping, local pickup, or both
7. Your Reddit username, which is shown on the listing as the way for buyers to contact you

Approved listings run for 30 days. Please send a follow-up email when your item sells so the listing can be marked as sold and removed. One listing per item, please.

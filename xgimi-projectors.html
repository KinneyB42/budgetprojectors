The brightness reality check

This is the big one. Compact projectors are dimmer than their full-size cousins — physics doesn't negotiate. In a dark room at 80–100 inches, they look great. With lamps on or daylight leaking in, the image washes out fast. If your room has ambient light you can't control, a brighter traditional projector is the better call.

What to check before buying

  • Lumens, honestly assessed: compare ANSI lumens, not marketing numbers, and assume you'll watch in the dark.
  • Battery vs. plug-in: battery models trade brightness and runtime for freedom — fine for a movie, not for all-day use.
  • Throw distance: compacts are usually standard throw; make sure your room fits the screen size you want (check here).
  • App support: verify the streaming apps you actually use are available and up to date on the built-in platform.

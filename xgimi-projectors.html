Buying Guide

XGIMI Projectors

XGIMI built its name on lifestyle projectors: compact, good-looking units with smart features and surprisingly decent built-in speakers. They are the "just works" option, great for bedrooms, apartments, and anyone who wants a projector without a rack of gear.

Where XGIMI shines

  • Portability: small enough to move room to room, or take to a friend's place. Auto-focus and auto-keystone mean setup takes seconds.
  • Built-in audio: tuned by real speaker brands on many models. Genuinely listenable, unlike most projector speakers.
  • Smart platform: streaming apps built in, so you may not need an external stick.

Brightness for real rooms

Brightness is still the spec to match to your room, but the old rule that compact projectors only belong in the dark is out of date. XGIMI's newer models are built for real living spaces, including rooms with lamps on and daylight coming in. The smaller portables are happiest at moderate screen sizes or with the lights lowered, while the brighter Horizon models are the ones to pick for bigger screens and brighter rooms. Choose the brightness for the room you actually watch in, and XGIMI has a model that fits it.

What to check before buying

  • Lumens, honestly assessed: compare ISO or ANSI lumens, not marketing numbers, then match the brightness to your screen size and how much light is usually in the room.
  • Battery vs. plug-in: battery models trade brightness and runtime for freedom. Fine for a movie, not for all-day use.
  • Throw distance: compacts are usually standard throw; make sure your room fits the screen size you want (check here).
  • App support: verify the streaming apps you actually use are available and up to date on the built-in platform.

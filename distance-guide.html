Distance Guide

Throw-Distance Calculator

Every projector throws a specific image size from a specific distance — that's the throw ratio. Get this wrong and your dream 120-inch screen becomes a 90-inch compromise. Run the numbers before you buy.

Common sizes: 80″, 100″, 120″. Assumes a 16:9 screen.

How throw ratio works

A projector's throw ratio (for example, 1.2x–1.5x) is the multiplier between image width and distance: a 1.5x throw needs 1.5 feet of distance for every foot of image width. Zoom lenses give you a range, which is why the calculator shows one. The calculator above uses the common 1.2x–1.5x span — always confirm your exact model's spec sheet for the precise figure.

Seating distance matters too

As a rule of thumb, sit about 1.5 to 2.5 times the screen width away. Too close and you'll see pixels and crane your neck; too far and you lose the cinematic feel. For a 100-inch screen (about 7.3 ft wide), that's roughly 11–18 ft of seating distance.

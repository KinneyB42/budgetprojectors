How throw ratio works

A projector's throw ratio (for example, 1.2x–1.5x) is the multiplier between image width and distance: a 1.5x throw needs 1.5 feet of distance for every foot of image width. Zoom lenses give you a range, which is why the calculator shows one. The calculator above uses your exact model's published throw ratio — pick it from the list and the math is done for you.

Seating distance matters too

As a rule of thumb, sit about 1.5 to 2.5 times the screen width away. Too close and you'll see pixels and crane your neck; too far and you lose the cinematic feel. For a 100-inch screen (about 7.3 ft wide), that's roughly 11–18 ft of seating distance.

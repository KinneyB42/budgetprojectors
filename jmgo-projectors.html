Buying Guide

JMGO Projectors

JMGO is the laser specialist. Triple laser color is the signature across most of the line, which means wide, rich color that LED portables cannot match, and the range runs from the flagship IRIS home theater models down through the N series gimbal portables to the pocket-size PicoFlix.

Where JMGO shines

  • Triple laser color: most models use a triple laser light source for a wide color gamut and a punchy image that holds up on big screens.
  • Gimbal design: the N series sits on a built-in gimbal stand that aims the image and auto-aligns it in seconds, no tripod or mount needed.
  • A real flagship tier: the IRIS line is built for home theater first, not portability, with the brightness and color to prove it.
  • Smart platform: Google TV built in on current models, so streaming apps are on board without an extra stick.

Brightness for real rooms

Brightness is still the spec to match to your room, but JMGO's current line is built for real living spaces, not just dark theater rooms. The IRIS models and the brighter N series hold up with lamps on and daylight in the room, especially at sensible screen sizes. The PicoFlix is the exception that proves the rule: it is a small-screen, dim-room portable. Pick the tier that matches your room and the line has you covered.

What to check before buying

  • Lumens, properly compared: compare ISO lumens, the standardized figure, then match the brightness to your screen size and how much light is usually in the room.
  • Throw distance: most JMGO models are standard throw; make sure your room fits the screen size you want (check here).
  • App support: verify the streaming apps you actually use are available and up to date on the built-in platform.
  • Audio: built-in speakers are decent across the line, but a soundbar still wins for movies (see our Audio Solutions guide).

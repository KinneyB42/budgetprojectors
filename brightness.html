How this works

Projector brightness only means something per square foot of screen. A 2,000-lumen projector on a 200-inch screen is dim; on an 80-inch screen it's blinding. This calculator multiplies your screen's area by a brightness target for your lighting, then adjusts for your screen's gain. Gray screens need more lumens; high-gain screens need fewer.

Two things the number doesn't tell you:

  • In a dark room, contrast beats brightness. Once you have enough lumens, deeper blacks matter far more than extra brightness. Don't overspend on lumens you'll never use.
  • Only compare ANSI lumens. "LED lumens" and "light source lumens" are inflated marketing numbers. If a listing won't say ANSI lumens, treat its brightness claim as fiction. See the glossary.

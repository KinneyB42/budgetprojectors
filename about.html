About Brandon

The Projector Guy

Brandon Kinney is the founder of r/BudgetProjectors — a community of more than 20,000 projector enthusiasts — and the person behind Budget Projectors, a site visited over 5 million times by people trying to buy the right projector the first time.

What started in 2016 as answering questions on Reddit turned into years of hands-on reviewing: testing projectors in real rooms, comparing specs against reality, and writing the kind of honest buying advice that's hard to find in a world of sponsored "top 10" lists.

Brandon's approach is room-first. The right projector depends on your space — its size, its light, your seating — more than on any spec sheet. That's the philosophy behind every guide on this site, and behind the personalized consultations he offers to readers who want a recommendation built around their exact setup.

When he's not calibrating something, he's in the community — answering questions on r/BudgetProjectors and Discord.

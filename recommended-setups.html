Recommended Setups

Start With Your Room

The room decides everything: brightness, screen size, audio. Pick the setup closest to yours and build from there.

Setup

Living Room

The shared family space: windows, lamps, and daytime viewing. Prioritize brightness and an ALR or gray screen to fight ambient light, keep the projector on a shelf or ceiling mount behind the seating, and add a soundbar for instant improvement. A pull-down screen keeps the room looking like a living room.

See the picks

Setup

Dedicated Theater

A light-controlled room is where projectors show what they can really do. Go big, 120 inches and up, with a fixed-frame matte white screen, a projector chosen for contrast and color accuracy rather than raw brightness, and a real surround setup. Treat first reflections and kill every light leak.

See the picks

Setup

Portable & Outdoor

Backyard movie nights and take-anywhere viewing. A bright portable projector, a tripod or inflatable screen, and a punchy portable speaker (wired in to avoid audio delay). Wait for full dark: even the brightest portable cannot beat sunset. Bring extension cords and bug spray.

See the picks

Setup

Gaming

Big-screen gaming lives or dies on input lag. Look for a projector with a dedicated low-lag game mode (under about 30ms), 1080p or 4K at 60Hz minimum, and brightness to match your room. Project the largest image your throw distance allows. Immersion is the whole point.
